about me

Last updated: August 2026

I grew up in Utah and somehow ended up studying materials science at a strange university called Duke Kunshan.

I want to build things that make civilization less stuck because I’ve seen what happens when medicine gets stuck. As a child, I lived with an incurable disease. Years later, I stood face-to-face with children facing the today uncurable as a researcher with the hands that could help.

I like strange ideas, beautiful machines, and people who take impossible things seriously.
